About Hon. Akarachi Amadi

Hon. Akarachi Amadi is a Nigerian politician representing Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency of Imo State in the Federal House of Representatives.

His legislative responsibilities include participating in lawmaking, national policy discussions and representing the interests and developmental needs of his constituents.

This website provides information about his biography, constituency, projects, legislative activities and public updates.
