Our Vision

To support a constituency where communities have improved access to opportunities, essential services and sustainable development, and where every person has the opportunity to contribute to progress.

✦

Our Mission

To provide responsive representation, listen to constituents, advocate for development priorities, support constructive legislation and promote accountability in public service.

Development Priorities

Areas of interest that can guide engagement and development discussions across Mbaitoli and Ikeduru.

Infrastructure

Supporting advocacy for better roads, public facilities, electricity and access to essential infrastructure.

Education

Promoting access to quality education, learning opportunities and skills development for young people.

Healthcare

Supporting efforts to improve access to healthcare services and community health facilities.

Youth Empowerment

Encouraging skills acquisition, entrepreneurship and opportunities that help young people participate in economic life.

Community Development

Encouraging inclusive engagement and attention to the needs of communities across both local government areas.

Responsive Representation

Listening to constituents, raising community concerns and communicating relevant updates about public responsibilities.

Representation Through Engagement

The needs and aspirations of constituents should remain central to representation. Community participation, constructive dialogue and accountability are important parts of the development process.
