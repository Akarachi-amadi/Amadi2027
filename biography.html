About Akarachi

Engr. Akarachi Etinosa Amadi is a Nigerian entrepreneur, philanthropist, chemical engineer and public servant whose journey reflects a commitment to enterprise, youth development, community advancement and public service.

An indigene of Eziama, Ikeduru Local Government Area of Imo State, Nigeria, Akarachi Amadi represents the Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency in the Federal House of Representatives, Abuja. His emergence into national politics represents a significant chapter in his journey of community engagement, leadership and service.

Early Life and Educational Background

Akarachi Etinosa Amadi was born in 1994 into the family of Prince Charles Chukwuemeka Amadi, popularly known as Charlvon.

He began his educational journey at Prime International School, Warri, Delta State, before proceeding to Straight Gate College, Remo, Ogun State, for his secondary education.

He subsequently gained admission into Covenant University, Ota, Ogun State, where he studied Chemical Engineering and obtained a Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.) degree.

Entrepreneurial and Professional Journey

Beyond his academic qualifications, Engr. Akarachi Amadi has developed interests in entrepreneurship and business development, particularly within the downstream oil and gas sector.

His professional engagements have extended into areas including offshore engineering support, catering services and other business ventures.

His entrepreneurial journey reflects an interest in creating opportunities, developing businesses and exploring practical solutions to economic challenges.

Humanitarian Services and Community Development

A defining aspect of Akarachi Amadi's public engagement is his involvement in humanitarian and community development initiatives.

Through the Akarachi Foundation, he has supported programmes and initiatives focused on education, youth empowerment, social welfare and community advancement.

His humanitarian activities have included:

  • Educational assistance and student support.
  • Support for indigent individuals and families.
  • Empowerment initiatives for market women.
  • Medical assistance for vulnerable people.
  • Community development interventions.
  • The Mbaike Unity Football Cup.

Political Journey and Legislative Service

Engr. Akarachi Etinosa Amadi's transition into politics reflects his interest in contributing to governance and representing the aspirations of his people.

In April 2023, following the supplementary election conducted by the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), he was declared the winner of the Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency election, securing 21,372 votes.

His victory brought him into the 10th House of Representatives of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, where he represents the interests of the people of Mbaitoli and Ikeduru.

His legislative responsibilities include representation, lawmaking and oversight, with the broader objective of contributing to national development and addressing issues affecting his constituency.

Vision and Commitment

Engr. Akarachi Amadi's public philosophy is rooted in the understanding that leadership should create opportunities, encourage participation and contribute to the improvement of people's lives.

His interests in entrepreneurship, humanitarian services, youth development and legislative representation reflect a broader commitment to community advancement.

He recognises the importance of investing in human capital, supporting small businesses, encouraging educational development and strengthening the social and economic foundations of communities.

His Commitment

"Building Opportunities, Empowering Communities and Advancing Representation."
