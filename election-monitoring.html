How the Monitoring Portal Works

  • Field personnel submit factual observations through the designated reporting channels.
  • Reports should include the relevant ward or polling unit, date and time.
  • Supporting evidence may be submitted where appropriate and safe.
  • Reports are reviewed before being published as verified information.
  • Unverified reports must not be presented as confirmed election results.
  • Official election results should always be obtained from INEC.

Our Commitment

The purpose of this portal is to support responsible documentation, transparency and access to election-related information. Participation must remain peaceful, lawful and respectful of voters, electoral officials and all political participants.
